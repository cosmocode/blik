Built by a founder who needed it

Thijs de Zoete runs Tinify, an image optimisation company. He wanted honest 360° feedback for his team. The tools he found cost €15,000 to €18,000 a year for 50 people, and kept the feedback on someone else's servers.

So he built Blik: open source, simple to run, with the price on the website. He uses it with his own team.
